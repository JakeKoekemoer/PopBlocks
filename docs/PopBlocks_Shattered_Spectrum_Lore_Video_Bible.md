# PopBlocks: The Shattered Spectrum

## Lore Video Production Bible

**Format:** Gemini clips up to 10 seconds, assembled in an editor\
**Visual style:** Cinematic clean-vector fantasy; deep navy `#081426`,
secondary navy `#101F36`; cyan `#00D4FF`, coral `#FF6B75`, yellow
`#FFC837`, purple `#986BFF`, lime `#7CFF68`; white highlights
`#F4F8FF`.\
**Spoiler policy:** Promotional video is spoiler-free. Character and
region films reveal the story progressively. Warden, Region 7, and
Region 8 contain major spoilers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. How to use this document

Generate each numbered clip separately, then assemble clips in order.
Most clips are 8--10 seconds. The voice lines are deliberately short.
For the cleanest results, generate narration separately and lay it over
the footage; AI video voices may change between clips.

**Continuity rules** - Reuse the same character reference images,
palette, aspect ratio, and global prompt. - If Gemini supports image
references, use the previous clip's final frame as the next clip's
reference. - **MATCH CUT:** align the next clip's first frame with the
previous clip's last frame. - **DISSOLVE:** overlap shots by roughly
6--12 frames. - **HARD CUT:** cut on the specified sound or movement. -
Add titles, subtitles, logos, and exact inscriptions in your editor. Do
not ask Gemini to render readable text. - Use one continuous music bed
per finished video, not a new music track for each clip. - Keep the
subject centred for later 16:9 and 9:16 versions. - The promo must not
reveal that the Warden deliberately shattered the Heart or show the
ending. - Atmospheric shots may be invented, but do not invent new plot
facts, powers, relationships, or canon dialogue.

## Global style prompt

Append this to every clip prompt:

> Cinematic clean-vector fantasy animation for an original mobile puzzle
> game, PopBlocks: The Shattered Spectrum. Deep midnight navy
> backgrounds (#081426), secondary blue-black surfaces (#101F36), crisp
> geometric silhouettes, glowing crystal edges, controlled bloom, subtle
> floating particles, elegant parallax, polished 2D/2.5D illustration,
> smooth deliberate camera movement, emotionally expressive but
> restrained. Spectrum palette: cyan #00D4FF, coral #FF6B75, yellow
> #FFC837, purple #986BFF, lime #7CFF68, white highlights #F4F8FF. No
> text, lettering, logos, watermarks, UI, photorealism, noisy detail, or
> rapid camera shake. Keep important subjects centred and leave negative
> space for titles added later.

## Canonical visual notes

-   **Spectrum:** five colours: cyan/Flow, coral/Courage, yellow/Spark,
    purple/Memory, lime/Growth. These are story associations only, not
    gameplay effects.
-   **Prism Heart:** a large faceted crystal that once anchored the
    world's colour balance. In old memories it is beautiful and steady;
    when failing, it is too bright at the centre and pulls colour
    inward; after the fracture it is fragmented.
-   **Pip:** small floating Spark, cyan-white glowing core, simple
    expressive face/silhouette and tiny light trails. Pip survived
    because it was far from the Heart when it broke and remembers
    feelings more than facts.
-   **Mara:** careful Crystal Keeper and historian; her arc is from
    defending the accepted account to questioning it.
-   **Bram:** warm, practical Tinkerer; he learns that some problems
    require trust, not another machine.
-   **Warden:** protective, weary, guarded. They were right to stop the
    overload but wrong to abandon the aftermath. Hide the face until the
    reveal.
-   **Restorer/player:** use a hand, silhouette, or first-person
    viewpoint; do not establish an unapproved appearance.
-   **Eight Prism fragments:** one milestone per region; they cannot be
    bought.

## Audio palette

Wonder: airy synth and glass chimes. Fading: sparse notes and distant
low pulse. Memory: crystal resonance and quiet mechanical hum. Fracture:
muted crystal groan, not a bomb sound. Restoration: separate notes
gradually joining into a warm harmony. Narration should be clear,
intimate, grounded, never shouty.
